Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile games listed below ("the Games", "we", "the apps") handle information when you use them. The same policy applies to:

• Bonbon Sort  (com.bonbonsort.app)
• Sprout Merge  (com.sproutmerge.app)

Information We Collect

The Games themselves do not require registration, login, or any personal information. We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise location data
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device

However, the Games display advertisements through Google AdMob, which collects limited technical data for ad delivery. See the "Advertising" section below for details.

Local Storage

Each Game saves your progress (level, coins, bonus items, settings, etc.) locally on your device using the browser's localStorage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any server
  • Is not shared with any third party
  • Can be deleted by clearing the app's storage

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games display advertisements served by Google AdMob. We use two ad formats:

  • Rewarded ads — shown only when you tap "Watch ad" yourself, in exchange for in-game bonuses (such as extra coins in Bonbon Sort or a bonus item in Sprout Merge).
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads — shown occasionally at natural pauses in gameplay (between completed levels or completed orders).

To deliver these ads, Google AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable, non-personal device identifier used by Google for ad personalization and frequency capping.
  • IP address — used to determine your approximate location (country/region) for ad targeting and fraud prevention.
  • Standard ad-interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked).

This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads policy.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads at any time in your device settings:
Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions).

Children's Privacy

The Games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All advertising is delivered through Google AdMob, which is configured to comply with Google's policies on family-appropriate content.

Permissions

The Games request only the Internet permission, used solely for loading advertisements through Google AdMob. The Games do not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive device functions.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, especially if we add new third-party services or change how data is used. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
