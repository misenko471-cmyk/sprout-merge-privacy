Privacy Policy Effective date: July 12, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile games listed below ("the Games", "we", "the apps") handle information when you use them. The same policy applies to: • Bonbon Sort (com.bonbonsort.app) • Sprout Merge (com.sproutmerge.app) Developer: AbsurdoxLab. Contact: absurdoxlab@gmail.com. Information We Do Not Collect The Games do not require registration or login. We do not collect, store, or transmit: Your name, email address, or phone number Precise location data (GPS) Photos, contacts, or files from your device Payment information (there are no in-app purchases) Information Collected by Google AdMob The Games display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects and shares the following data types with Google: Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable, non-personal device identifier. Used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Approximate location — derived from your IP address (country/region only, not GPS). Used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. App interactions — anonymous ad-interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked). Used for analytics and fraud prevention. Diagnostics — technical performance data from the AdMob SDK (ad load latency, error rates). Used for analytics and fraud prevention. All data is transmitted encrypted over HTTPS to Google servers. It is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads policy. Ad formats used Rewarded ads — shown only when you tap "Watch ad" yourself, in exchange for in-game bonuses (extra coins in Bonbon Sort, bonus items in Sprout Merge). Interstitial ads — shown occasionally at natural pauses in gameplay (between completed levels or completed orders). Local Storage Each Game saves your progress (level, coins, bonus items, settings, etc.) locally on your device using the browser's localStorage. This data: Never leaves your device Is not transmitted to any server Is not shared with any third party How to Request Data Deletion We do not maintain our own servers and we do not store any personal data linked to you. Data related to advertising is held by Google. Here is how to delete or request deletion of that data: 1. Delete local game data Uninstall the Game, or clear its storage in your Android settings: Settings → Apps → Bonbon Sort / Sprout Merge → Storage → Clear Data 2. Reset your Advertising ID This removes the identifier used by Google AdMob to link ad data to your device: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions). 3. Request Google to delete AdMob-side data Contact Google directly through their privacy portal: policies.google.com/privacy. Google honors deletion requests for data associated with your Advertising ID. 4. Contact us If you have questions or need help with the steps above, contact us at absurdoxlab@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Because we do not hold any personal data on our side, we cannot delete data we do not have — but we will help you use Google's tools. Data Retention Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained by Google according to their policies for as long as necessary for the purposes described above (advertising, analytics, fraud prevention). See Google's privacy portal for details on their retention periods. Children's Privacy The Games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All advertising is delivered through Google AdMob, which is configured to comply with Google's policies on family-appropriate content. Permissions The Games request only the Internet permission (for loading advertisements) and the AD_ID permission (used by Google AdMob to serve personalized ads). The Games do not request access to your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or any other sensitive device functions.
