Privacy Policy Effective date: September 6, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile games listed below ("the Games", "we", "the apps") handle information when you use them. The same policy applies to: • Bonbon Sort (com.bonbonsort.app) • Sprout Merge (com.sproutmerge.app) Developer: AbsurdoxLab. Contact: absurdoxlab@gmail.com. Information We Do Not Collect The Games do not require registration or login. We do not collect, store, or transmit: Your name, email address, or phone number Precise location data (GPS) Photos, contacts, or files from your device Payment information — we never receive or store your card or billing details In-App Purchases Bonbon Sort has no in-app purchases at all. Sprout Merge offers one optional one-time purchase: Remove ads, which disables the interstitial ads shown between orders. Rewarded videos remain available and are watched only at your choice. The purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was completed — no payment details are shared with us. Your purchase is restored automatically when you reinstall the game or sign in on another device with the same Google account. Information Collected by Our Advertising Partners The Games display advertisements. In Bonbon Sort they are served by Google AdMob. In Sprout Merge they are served by Google AdMob, the Yandex Advertising Network or Unity Ads — whichever network has a suitable ad available at that moment; availability differs by country. The app does not determine your country and shares nothing about it beyond the data listed below. Any of these networks collects and shares the following data types with itself: Advertising ID (AAID) — a resettable, non-personal device identifier. Used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Approximate location — derived from your IP address (country/region only, not GPS). Used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. App interactions — anonymous ad-interaction data (whether an ad was shown, viewed, or clicked). Used for analytics and fraud prevention. Diagnostics — technical performance data from the AdMob SDK (ad load latency, error rates). Used for analytics and fraud prevention. All data is transmitted encrypted over HTTPS to the network’s own servers. It is processed under that network’s policy: Google (see also the Google Ads policy), Yandex, Unity. Ad formats used Rewarded ads — shown only when you tap "Watch ad" yourself, in exchange for in-game bonuses (extra coins in Bonbon Sort, bonus items in Sprout Merge). Interstitial ads — shown occasionally at natural pauses in gameplay (between completed levels or completed orders). Google Play Games Services (Sprout Merge only) Sprout Merge offers optional leaderboards ("Village help" and "Best garden") powered by Google Play Games Services. Using them is entirely your choice — the rest of the game works without signing in. If you sign in, your Play Games profile (the gamer name and avatar you chose in Google Play Games) and your scores — the number of orders you completed and the highest plant level you grew — are sent to Google and shown in the public leaderboards. We do not receive your Google account name, email address, or any other personal data. Leaderboard entries are stored and displayed by Google, not by us. You can hide your profile, delete your leaderboard scores, or delete your Play Games data at any time in the Google Play Games app: Profile → Settings. Instructions and controls are provided by Google at support.google.com/googleplay. This data is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy. Local Storage Each Game saves your progress (level, coins, bonus items, settings, etc.) locally on your device. This data: Never leaves your device Is not transmitted to any server of ours Is not shared with any third party Leaderboard scores, if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, are the only game data that leaves your device — see the section above. How to Request Data Deletion We do not maintain our own servers and we do not store any personal data linked to you. Data related to advertising is held by the ad networks. Here is how to delete or request deletion of that data: 1. Delete local game data Uninstall the Game, or clear its storage in your Android settings: Settings → Apps → Bonbon Sort / Sprout Merge → Storage → Clear Data 1a. Delete leaderboard data (Sprout Merge) Open the Google Play Games app → Profile → Settings, where you can hide your profile from leaderboards or delete your Play Games data for this game. This data is held by Google; we have no way to edit or delete it on your behalf. 2. Reset your Advertising ID The advertising identifier is shared by all the networks, so resetting it breaks the link to your device for every one of them at once: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on older Android versions). 3. Ask the ad networks to delete their data Contact the network whose data you want removed: Google, Yandex, Unity. Each of them honors deletion requests for data associated with your Advertising ID. 4. Contact us If you have questions or need help with the steps above, contact us at absurdoxlab@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Because we do not hold any personal data on our side, we cannot delete data we do not have — but we will help you use Google's tools. Data Retention Local game data is retained on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Data collected by the ad networks is retained by them according to their own policies for as long as necessary for the purposes described above (advertising, analytics, fraud prevention). See each network's privacy portal, linked above, for details on their retention periods. Children's Privacy The Games are suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All advertising is delivered through Google AdMob, the Yandex Advertising Network and Unity Ads, each configured to comply with its own policies on family-appropriate content. Permissions The Games request only the Internet permission (for loading advertisements and, in Sprout Merge, for leaderboards) and the AD_ID permission (used by the ad networks to serve personalized ads). The Games do not request access to your camera, microphone, precise location, contacts, or any other sensitive device functions.
